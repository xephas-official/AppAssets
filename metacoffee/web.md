# Project Requirements Document: Custom Coffee E-Commerce Platform

## 1. Project Overview

The platform allows customers to order custom-roasted coffee by selecting a country, choosing a coffee type (Arabica or Robusta), selecting a local roastery and specific roaster, and customizing their roast preferences.

---

## 2. Customer Order & Customization Flow

•⁠  ⁠*Step 1: Select Country*
•⁠  ⁠Display available countries (e.g., Rwanda, Uganda).

•⁠  ⁠Selecting a country dynamically loads the coffee types and roasteries operating within that country.

•⁠  ⁠*Step 2: Select Coffee Type & Quantity*
•⁠  ⁠*Coffee Type:* Choose between *Arabica* or *Robusta*.
•⁠  ⁠*Available Coffee:* Select the specific coffee variety available.

•⁠  ⁠*Quantity:* Specify the amount in kilograms (kgs).

•⁠  ⁠*Step 3: Select Roastery & Roaster*
•⁠  ⁠*Roastery:* Choose a roastery located in the selected country (the roastery's location is displayed automatically).

•⁠  ⁠*Roaster:* Choose a specific individual/roaster at that roastery to handle the roasting.

•⁠  ⁠*Step 4: Custom Roast Profile*
•⁠  ⁠*Roast Profile:* Select the desired roast level (e.g., Light, Medium, Dark).

---

## 3. Checkout & Fulfillment

•⁠  ⁠*Step 5: Pricing & Charge*
•⁠  ⁠Display total cost/amount based on selected coffee, roaster, and quantity.

•⁠  ⁠*Step 6: Delivery & Final Payment*
•⁠  ⁠*Delivery Point:* Drop-off address/location.

•⁠  ⁠*Delivery Time:* Preferred delivery time slot.

•⁠  ⁠*Final Amount:* Total summary including delivery charges.
